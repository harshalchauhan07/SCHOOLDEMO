QUICK ANSWERS

Admission FAQs

How can I apply for admission?

Click "Apply Now", fill the form or call us on +91 90154 70442. Our office will guide you on documents and dates.

Which classes are open for admission?

Admissions are open from Nursery to the senior classes, subject to seat availability.

What documents are required?

Birth certificate, previous school report card, transfer certificate (if applicable), Aadhaar card and passport-size photographs.

Is school transport available?

Yes, bus service is available for nearby areas. Please ask the school office about routes and fees.
